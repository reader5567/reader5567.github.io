One of 35 people selected for the Lead for America National Hometown Fellowship, an AmeriCorps program that places young professionals in public service roles in their hometown communities.

- Conducted a year-long listening tour, interviewing over 150 county stakeholders to understand their needs and creating a report for the County Commissioners
- Spearheaded research into equity issues around the county, including a survey of 1,100 staff members, that culminated in the creation of the county's first full-time DEI Manager position
- Facilitated community forums and advised commissioners on organizational planning and diverse hiring pipelines
