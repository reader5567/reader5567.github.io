### Fiction

- [*This Is How You Lose the Time War*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Is_How_You_Lose_the_Time_War) by [Amal El-Mohtar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amal_El-Mohtar) and [Max Gladstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Gladstone)
  My favorite book of all time. It's a lyrical, poignant love story that contains one of the most beautiful descriptions of love I've ever read.
- [*Piranesi*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piranesi_(novel)) by [Susanna Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susanna_Clarke)
  A quick, fun read about a 'lil guy wandering a mysterious house, and I'm afraid I can't share more than that!
- [*Venomous Lumpsucker*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venomous_Lumpsucker) by [Ned Beauman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ned_Beauman)
  An engaging work of climate fiction with more than a little bit of that classic British self-deprecation.

### Non-fiction

- [*Why Fish Don't Exist*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lulu_Miller) by [Lulu Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lulu_Miller)
  A book about the incredible life and disturbing contradictions of [David Starr Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Starr_Jordan). I started reading this on my break at work and was so enthralled that I stayed three hours late to finish it.
- [*We Wish to Inform You That Tomorrow We Will Be Killed with Our Families*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Wish_to_Inform_You_That_Tomorrow_We_Will_Be_Killed_with_Our_Families) by [Philip Gourevitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Gourevitch)
  A narrative of the [Rwandan genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwandan_genocide). This book was assigned reading in my first year of undergrad and it formed the foundation of my life's trajectory.
- *Cross-X* by Joe Miller
  Follows the [Central High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_High_School_(Kansas_City,_Missouri)) speech and debate team in their journey to the 2002 National Speech and Debate Tournament. As someone whose life was changed by participation in speech and debate, I found this book deeply moving.

### Purely academic

- *Two Dozen (or so) Arguments for God* by [Jerry Walls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Walls) and Trent Dougherty
  A long-form articulation of [Alvin Plantinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alvin_Plantinga)'s arguments for God's existence, with a team of contributors that includes [Alexander Pruss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Pruss), [William Lane Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lane_Craig), and [Richard Swinburne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Swinburne).
- *Washington's End* by Jonathan Horn
  An unusual biography that focuses almost entirely on [George Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington)'s post-presidency years, providing insight into his personal struggle with slavery.
- *Normal Life* by [Dean Spade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Spade)
  About the myriad of ways bureaucratic violence impacts the life of trans people in America and around the world.
