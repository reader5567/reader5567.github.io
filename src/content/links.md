This is a big collection of things I like, find interesting, recommend, and/or actively use. I hope you do too!

## Dailies
- [Framed](https://framed.wtf/)
  A daily movie trivia game from still shots and posters.
- [Geoguessr](https://www.geoguessr.com/)
  The original find-out-where-you-are game.
- [Krillion](https://krillion.io/)
  A trivia game where rarer answers sink you deeper into the ocean.
- [Lessgames](https://lessgames.com/)
  A collection of five lightweight dailies.
- [Patches](https://www.linkedin.com/games/patches/)
  Fill in a grid with a set of shapes.
- [Timeguessr](https://timeguessr.com/)
  Find out when (and where) you are.
- [Wordle](https://www.nytimes.com/games/wordle/index.html)
  Six chances to guess a single five-letter word.
- [Zip](https://www.linkedin.com/games/zip/)
  Fill in a grid with a single line (no backtracks).
## Favorites
- [*Antilibraries*](https://www.antilibrari.es/)
  One of my favorite "hidden gem" websites: a collection of books that have yet to be read. It inspired me to create my own antilibrary, which quickly just became my actual library.
- [The Hunt for the Death Valley Germans](https://otherhand.org/home-page/search-and-rescue/the-hunt-for-the-death-valley-germans/)
  This is a truly sprawling series of blog posts by Tom Mahood, an engineer and casual blogger who discovered the bodies of a German family who went missing in Death Valley in 1996.
- [Lost Media Wiki](https://lostmediawiki.com/Home)
  A very cool website where people with way too much time on their hands track down things that have gone missing.
- [Tiny Awards](https://tinyawards.net/)
  An annual award ceremony for the most creative handmade sites on the internet.
- [Websites Done Cheap](https://cheap.urls.loan/)
  Also linked in the footer of this site, but [Eli Beaton](https://elijah.beaton.page/) is a master designer and I am very, very grateful that I learned of his cheap website service before his prices became slightly-less-cheap.

## Reading

### Blogs

- [ANVHN](https://anhvn.com/blog/)
  A very sweet blog by a Canadian designer, mostly keeping track of media and life.
- [Astral Codex Ten](https://www.astralcodexten.com/)
  The evolution of Slate Star Codex. Helpful for keeping up with futurism news, although he tends to float a little too close to some right-wing ideologies for my taste.
- [Eclectic Orthodoxy](https://afkimel.wordpress.com/)
  Fr. Adrian Kimel's blog, my favorite religion website and an invaluable resource for Christian Universalism.
- [File 770](https://file770.com/)
  A long-running fanzine by [Mike Glyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Glyer) and associates, which was my introduction to the wonderful world of fandom.
- [Joel Chrono](https://joelchrono.xyz/)
  A very much stream-of-consciousness blog from a Mexican sysadmin and hobbyist.
- [Web Curios](https://webcurios.co.uk/)
  A bi-weekly roundup of unique corners on the internet (and where I discovered many of the links on this page).
### Internet fiction
- [17776](https://www.sbnation.com/a/17776-football)
  A sprawling, speculative narrative about what football will look like in the far future.
- [Mystery Flesh Pit National Park](https://www.mysteryfleshpitnationalpark.com/) 
  A fascinating work of cosmic horror worldbuilding by Trevor Roberts, which feels all too plausible in today's America. 
- [The SCP Foundation](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/)
  The single greatest work of collaborative fiction to have ever existed. Now pushing 10,000 articles!
### News
- [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site)
  A worker-owned news organization covering internet culture, run by laid-off staffers from the once-great websites Kotaku and Vice.
- [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/)
  Technology news from (as the title suggests) the rest of the world.
- [Rock Paper Shotgun](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/)
  A PC game review site that covers AAA titles and indie releases in equal measure.
### Periodicals
- [The Comics Journal](https://www.tcj.com/)
  A place for more intelligent comics criticism than the average pop culture-oriented website.
- [The Drift](https://www.thedriftmag.com/)
  A magazine of cultural criticism, narrative nonfiction, and short stories.
- [Harper's Magazine](https://harpers.org/)
  The oldest, and most consistently interesting, magazine in the United States.
- [The HTML Review](https://thehtml.review)
  A regular online publication of literature designed for the internet, in all the beautiful forms that can take.
- [The New Atlantis](https://www.thenewatlantis.com/)
  A pro-human journal of science and technology. About as conservative as I'm willing to go these days.
- [The New York Review of Books](https://www.nybooks.com/)
  A regular magazine covering literature, culture, economics, science, politics, and everything in between.
- [Noema](https://www.noemamag.com/)
  A publication of the [Berggruen Institute](https://berggruen.org/) that releases longform journalism at the intersection of technology, philosophy, and law.
## Software
### Desktop

- [Darktable](https://www.darktable.org/)
  An open source RAW photo editor with virtually unlimited customizations. If you're not taking highly stylized photos, I highly recommend it.
- [Digikam](https://www.digikam.org/)
  A free digital asset manager that makes pruning the hundreds of photos I take into a significantly more usable amount.
- [Firefox](https://www.firefox.com/en-US/)
  Has been my browser of choice since I was 12 years old. Non-profit, open source, and privacy-first.
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)
  A fantastic free markdown editor that all the pages of this site are written in and then pushed live with the [Git](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-git) plugin.
- [Pangram](https://www.pangram.com/)
  Not quite software but close enough. The most reliable of the AI detectors, and although I think my AI-slop-o-meter is quite high, it's always nice to have confirmation.
### Extensions

- [Bypass Paywalls Clean](https://gitflic.ru/project/magnolia1234/bypass-paywalls-firefox-clean)
  A very nifty tool for bypassing paywalls on many news sites.
- [Feedbro](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/feedbroreader/)
  A lightweight RSS reader for Firefox that I use to keep up with many of the blogs and publications on this page.
- [Indie Wiki Buddy](https://getindie.wiki/)
  A project of [Kevin Payravi](https://kevin.payravi.dev/) that redirects links from corporate-owned fan sites to independent alternatives.
- [Old Reddit Redirect](https://github.com/tom-james-watson/old-reddit-redirect)
  A lightweight extension that automatically converts reddit.com links to old.reddit.com.
- [RECAP](https://free.law/recap/)
  An extension that automatically uploads any files purchased on PACER to the RECAP Archive, thereby making them permanently publicly available.
- [Reddit Enhancement Suite](https://redditenhancementsuite.com/)
  An extension with massive quality-of-life improvements to Reddit.
- [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app/)
  An open source, crowdsourced database of sponsor segments in YouTube videos that automatically skips ad reads.
- [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock#ublock-origin)
  The best ad blocker for Firefox.
